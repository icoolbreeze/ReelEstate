# Footage naming & placement (map-hub 口播)

## Where
- `projects/map-hub/<ep>/footage/raw/` — the user's own shot footage. **Read-only** for agents (never edit, move or delete).
- `projects/map-hub/<ep>/footage/<ID>.mp4` — working clips used by the template (Muse output or trimmed copies of raw clips).
- `projects/map-hub/<ep>/footage/heygen/HG*.mp4` — split HeyGen segments; `HG00_alpha.webm` = matted hook.
- Muse prompts/manifest: `<ep>/muse_prompts/`. Never put footage in `templates/` or another episode.

## Raw naming rule: `房间_序号.mp4`
- 房间 = one of 客厅, 主卧, 次卧, 卧室, 书房, 厨房, 卫生间, 餐厅, 阳台, 玄关, 外立面, 小区, 大堂, 周边 (Chinese, no spaces).
- 序号 = 1, 2, 3 … per room in shooting order. Examples: `客厅_1.mp4`, `客厅_2.mp4`, `厨房_1.mp4`, `小区_1.mp4`.
- .mp4 or .mov; landscape, ≥ 1080p preferred, 4–15 s, steady (gimbal/slow pan), no people's faces, no plate numbers.
- `scripts/new_episode.py --footage <dir>` copies only correctly named files and lists the rest.

## Mapping to template clip IDs
| room | clip IDs | map card |
|---|---|---|
| 客厅 | LR1, LR2 | room_living |
| 主卧/卧室/次卧 | BR1, BR2 | room_bed |
| 厨房/卫生间 | KB1, KB2 | room_kb |
| 小区/外立面/大堂 | ENV1–ENV3 | env |
| 周边 | NB1–NB3, MET1 | POI cut-aways |
Raw clips are trimmed/converted to `footage/<ID>.mp4` (ffmpeg, 30 fps, scale to ≥ 1088×816 4:3 crop-safe). Rooms without raw
footage are generated by Muse (episode.yaml `footage.muse: auto`); generic Muse B-roll is narrated "画面仅作示意".
