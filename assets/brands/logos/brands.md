# 贝壳平台 / 贝联品牌与 logo 来源（2026-10-09 整理）

“贝联”是贝壳财报里的口径：贝壳平台上**链家以外**的经纪品牌、门店和经纪人统称“贝联”（链家是贝壳直营品牌）。
贝壳没有公开完整的贝联品牌名单，截至 2024 年末平台接入的非链家经纪品牌约 266 个（国盛证券研报引用）。下表只列有可靠公开来源的品牌。

## 中环（中环地产，贝壳平台品牌，2019-08 加入）
- Order in strip/outro: 链家, 德佑, 富房, CENTURY 21, 中环.
- Source: logo image supplied by the user 2026-10-09 (2025 identity: red rounded-square mark + 「中环地产」), saved as
  `original/zhonghuan_user_supplied_watermarked.png` (1050×758, white bg, light diagonal 「设计无忧网」 watermark from the
  download site). Official background: 中环 brand upgrade 2025-06-20 (hea.china.com/articles/20250623/202506231689569.html).
- Extraction (uncropped, 6 px margin): alpha from darkness for the black wordmark (watermark lum > ~0.8 drops to 0) and from
  saturation for the red mark; red mask closed/filled to remove watermark streaks; specks < 150 px dropped.
  `zhonghuan_color.png` (red #D60F21 mark + near-black text, transparent) and `zhonghuan_mono.png` (alpha mask for the strip), 612×135, ar 4.5333.
